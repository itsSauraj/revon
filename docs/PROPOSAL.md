# A proposal to add a temporary, link-based 'Group Convoy Mode' in Google Maps that lets 10-25 drivers

Feature Request: "Group Convoy Mode" for Multi-Vehicle Navigation 
The Problem:
When groups of 10–25 drivers or riders travel together on trips, keeping track of everyone is dangerous and tedious. Users constantly have to pull over, check fragmented third-party apps like WhatsApp Live Location, or call each other to ask, "Where are you?" and "Are you ahead or behind me?"
The Solution (Group Convoy Mode):
A native, temporary, peer-to-peer group navigation feature within Google Maps that displays all group members on a single shared map interface during a trip. 
User Flow:

   1. Initiate: A user sets a destination and taps a new "Start Group Ride" button next to the standard navigation start button.
   2. Invite: The initiator gets a temporary group invite link at the bottom of the screen to share via any messaging app.
   3. Decentralized Control: There is no strict host. Anyone who joins via the link can invite remaining group members.
   4. Active Session: Once a member joins, their custom map marker (with their name/initials) appears on everyone's screen in real time. The session automatically ends for a user when they reach the destination or manually tap "Leave Group."

Key Features & Technical Details:

* Proximity & Telemetry Insights: Clicking a friend's marker displays their current speed, status (e.g., "Stopped" or "Moving"), and their estimated time/distance away from your current position.
* Smart Notifications (Silent & Persistent):
* A silent notification triggers when a driver safely reaches the destination.
   * A proactive alert triggers if a driver takes a wrong turn or changes their route.
   * A "Fuel/Rest Stop" icon that a user can toggle to signal a pit stop to the whole group.
* Emergency SOS Button: A dedicated "Need Help" button that instantly alerts all group members, flashes their marker red, and automatically routes everyone to the distressed driver's location if requested.
* Seamless Contact (In-App Calling): Users can tap a group member's profile marker to initiate a call.
* Privacy & Platform Execution: To protect privacy while keeping cross-platform (Android/iOS) functionality simple, users explicitly consent to share their phone number with the temporary group upon joining, or Google Maps can leverage Google Account-linked phone numbers. Because convoy members are real-world friends/acquaintances traveling together, this permission boundaries logic is highly acceptable to the end-user.

Why Google Maps Should Build This:
This feature drives massive user engagement, keeps eyes on the road (improving safety), and blocks competitors from capturing the group travel market. It transforms Google Maps from an individual tool into a social, community-driven utility
